Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Auto Video Maker Publisher handles information when you use the application on your own computer.

Overview

Auto Video Maker Publisher is a desktop-based application for creating short-form educational videos and, after explicit authorization, publishing user-selected videos through official social media platform APIs. This policy explains what information the application may handle and how it is treated.

Account Authorization and Tokens

  • The application may use OAuth authorization tokens to connect an authorized social media account.
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally by the application.
  • Tokens are not intentionally sold or shared with third parties.

Account Information

Basic account information may be accessed only when required by an authorized platform API integration. The application does not access unrelated third-party accounts.

Video Files and Publishing Metadata

Video files and publishing metadata are processed for the purpose of publishing content selected by the user.

Information Transmitted to Platforms

Information may be transmitted to the relevant social media platform when the user explicitly initiates an authorized publishing action.

No Sale of Information

Information is not sold for advertising or marketing purposes.

User Responsibility

Users remain responsible for content they create and publish through the application.
